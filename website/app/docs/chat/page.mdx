# Chat

Any signed-in account can chat. The Chat section shows your rooms from the server, kept up to date over `/sync`.

<Media src="chat.gif" alt="Chatting from the phantom console" caption="Someone typing, a message sent, then switching rooms." />

## Rooms

Rooms are listed newest activity first, direct messages after them. A room is named by its name, then its alias, then its members. The members panel shows who is online and who is an admin of the room.

## Reading

The timeline shows messages with edits applied, reactions counted, thread replies counted on their root, deleted messages marked, and joins, invites, bans, renames and topic changes as sentences. Opening a room moves your read receipt to its newest message.

## Writing

<Kbd>enter</Kbd> starts writing, <Kbd>enter</Kbd> again sends, <Kbd>esc</Kbd> stops. Others see you typing while you write. A message shows at once as *sending…* until the server confirms it, or *not sent* with the reason. Start with `/me ` for an emote.

## Joining and leaving

- `:join #room:server` joins a room by alias or ID and opens it.
- `:leave` leaves the open room, after asking.

<Callout>
The console cannot decrypt end-to-end encrypted rooms; their messages say so. Invites aren't listed — join the room with `:join`.
</Callout>
